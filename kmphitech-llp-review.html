Kmphitech LLP review — the key facts

I commissioned Kmphitech LLP to build a shrimp-farm management application and web administration panel. Their written proposal, dated 2 June 2025, described the project as a mobile application and web panel development project and stated a total timeline of 56 business days.

The project did not conclude within anything close to that original timeframe. Project records show development, testing, deployment and further issue resolution continuing into September 2026. That is the central issue documented on this page.

Central timeline fact: the proposal stated 56 business days; project activity continued from June 2025 into September 2026.

I want to distinguish the timeline issue from the question of whether every individual bug was ultimately fixed. Some items were fixed and the applications were eventually uploaded. My concern is the overall time, effort, repeated follow-up, changing milestones and prolonged delivery process required to reach that point.

What Kmphitech LLP originally promised

The original written proposal is the clearest reference point because it sets out the intended application and development deliverables. The scope included:

Application functionality

  • Farm and pond management
  • Multiple farms and ponds
  • Role-based access
  • Farm Owner and Employee access
  • Assign ponds to employees
  • Farming-cycle initialization
  • Mid-cycle/backdated entry
  • Four daily feeding slots
  • Automatic feed consumption tracking
  • Biomass tracking
  • Sampling records
  • FCR calculation
  • Growth monitoring / ADG

Reporting & technical delivery

  • Per-farm and per-pond reports
  • Daily/weekly/monthly filters
  • PDF/CSV exports
  • Admin panel
  • Android application
  • iOS application
  • Web panel
  • Backend APIs
  • Database
  • Frontend/backend integration
  • Testing
  • App-store deployment

Post-launch problems and continued work

The original proposal itself included one month of remote support after Go-Live. The later project record shows that issue resolution continued after the applications had reached the stores.

Important distinction: the existence of post-launch fixes is not presented here as proof that the product was never delivered. It is documented to show that project activity continued long after the original delivery commitment.

26 September 2026: production issue discovered during actual use

I reported that a problem had reappeared after previously being fixed. Mitul responded that the team believed I was already using the delivered application and had not known the issue was occurring.

27 September 2026: root cause identified

The backend team explained that numerical time-field data was arriving as strings, causing a string-versus-number comparison problem. They stated that this caused the data to be stored as zero and that they corrected the data handling.

29 September 2026: store review still ongoing

The latest available message in the project record states that the Play Console build was approved while the App Store build was still waiting for review.

My experience with Kmphitech LLP

My principal concern is the overall project experience rather than one isolated bug. I spent a substantial amount of time following up on deadlines, reviewing builds, providing feedback, testing functionality and trying to keep the project moving.

In April 2026 I formally explained that the project was approximately ten months into a contract stated as 56 business days. I also described repeated deadline commitments, communication gaps and the amount of additional effort I had personally contributed to help clarify the required product.

The company responded by saying that the early June, July and August milestones had been completed within agreed timelines with one four-day extension, while also explaining that later time had been spent on KPI discussions and iterations. The response proposed another 1–2 working days once I supplied a final calculation logic.

My view is that the central issue remains the overall elapsed time and the repeated need for follow-up. The project began in June 2025 with a 56-business-day commitment and continued into September 2026.

This is a first-hand review. I am describing one project and my own experience. I am not claiming that every customer of Kmphitech LLP has had the same experience.
